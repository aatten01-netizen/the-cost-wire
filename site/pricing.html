Questions

Is Pro open now?

Not yet. The free brief is live today. Pro opens once its features are built. Nothing on this page charges you.

Where do the numbers come from?

Public USDA AMS, USDA NASS and EIA reports. Each number shows its source link and date. If a source is missing, the number is left out.

Will you tell me when to buy fertilizer?

No. The Cost Wire reports published prices and how they changed. It is data, not advice. Buying decisions are yours.

Can I cancel?

Yes, Pro will be month to month (or yearly) with no contract.
