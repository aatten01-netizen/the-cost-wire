Rules kept: data, not advice; no subscriber counts, testimonials or income claims; no prizes or paid rewards (nothing to buy, nothing to pay out); no FFA/schools/colleges.

## The offer (plain)
The Cost Wire is free. The "reward" for forwarding it is also free and costs Andy nothing:
**Forward the Monday brief to one neighbor, landlord, lender or agronomist who prices inputs. If they sign up and reply with your name, you both get a personal thank-you from Andy and a first look at the paid tier (your-state numbers, price alerts, cost-per-acre calculator) when it opens.**
No points, no gift cards, no contests (those need rules, tax forms and money). No promise of a price or a date for the paid tier; BRIEF.md has no launch date yet.

Why this fits the buyer: farmers already share input prices at the elevator, co-op counter and kitchen table. A sourced page is easy to forward because it isn't anyone's opinion.
