# Iowa and Illinois fertilizer prices vs last year

Every fall the same question comes up: is fertilizer higher or lower than a year ago? The answer is in public USDA reports. Here is where the numbers come from, what the latest report says, and how to check last year yourself.

## Where the numbers come from
USDA's Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) publishes a bi-weekly Production Cost Report for each state. Iowa's is report 2863 and Illinois's is report 3195. They list average prices reported by retailers, in dollars per ton for fertilizer and dollars per gallon for fuel.

- Iowa: https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_2863.pdf
- Illinois: https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_3195.pdf

These are averages across reporting dealers, not a quote. Your dealer's price can be higher or lower.

## How to find last year's price
1. Open the state report page on USDA's Market News site (search "Iowa Production Cost Report" or "Illinois Production Cost Report" at https://mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov/).
2. Choose the report for the same two-week window last year (late October 2025).
3. Read the same line, for example anhydrous ammonia, and write down the price and report date.
4. Subtract: this year minus last year, then divide by last year for the percent.

Check the exact click path on the site before publishing; we could not confirm it from the build environment.

## Why the same product can differ between states
Each report averages different dealers and uses its own table layout. Compare Iowa to Iowa and Illinois to Illinois year over year. A gap between the two states is not a like-for-like comparison.

## What the numbers do not tell you
They do not include delivery, application, volume discounts or prepay terms. They are not a forecast.
