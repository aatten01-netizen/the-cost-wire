# Unsubscribe form + one-question exit survey

Ready to paste into the free Google Form from `docs/unsubscribe.md`. Purpose: make leaving easy (it's the law, and it keeps trust), and learn why people leave so the brief gets better. No guilt-trip, no "are you sure?" screens.

## Form title
Unsubscribe from The Cost Wire

## Form description
Sorry to see you go. Put your email below and you won't get The Cost Wire anymore (weekly brief and any price alerts). It can take a few days to clear.

## Question 1 (required, short answer)
Your email

## Question 2 (optional, multiple choice) — "Mind telling us why? (optional)"
- Too many emails
- My state's prices aren't in it
- I already get these numbers somewhere else
- The numbers come out too late to help me
- I'm not buying inputs right now
- Other: ____

## Confirmation message (Settings > Presentation)
Done. You're off the list. If you ever want the weekly input-cost brief again, it's free at https://aatten01-netizen.github.io/the-cost-wire/ . Thanks for reading.

## How to use the answers
Once a month, count the reasons. If "my state isn't in it" leads, that's the next state to add; if "too late" leads, look at sending earlier. Never email someone who unsubscribed to ask again.
