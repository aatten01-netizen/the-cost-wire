# Pro "would you pay?" check (asset 12)

**Goal:** BRIEF.md says build the paid tier after 5 farmers say they'd pay for their state + alerts. This is the cheapest way to find out: one short email to people who already read the free brief, answered by plain reply. No form, no tool, no cost.

**Send to:** free brief readers only (people who signed up), after the signup form is live. Never cold lists. Never schools, colleges or FFA.
**Send how:** Andy sends it from his own inbox (or as a P.S. in the Monday brief). Bots don't send it. Reply SEND in chat to have it queued for your approval.

---

**Subject:** Quick question about The Cost Wire (2 minutes)

Hi,

Thanks for reading The Cost Wire. I'm deciding what to build next and would rather ask than guess. Just hit reply and answer what you want to:

1. Which state do you farm in?
2. Which input matters most to you right now: anhydrous, urea, UAN, phosphate/potash, diesel, propane, feed, or cash rent?
3. Would an email alert when that published price moves by an amount you pick be useful? (yes / no / maybe)
4. Would a brief built for your state, plus those alerts and a cost-per-acre worksheet, be worth $9 a month to you? (yes / no / at a different price: ___)
5. Anything in the brief you'd change?

Every answer gets read by me. The free brief stays free either way.

Andy
The Cost Wire. Data, not advice.

---

**How to count (be honest):**
- Count only a clear "yes" to question 4 as a "would pay". "Maybe" doesn't count.
- Goal from BRIEF.md: 5 clear yeses before building Pro alerts. Write the tally (numbers only, no names) in LOG.md.
- Keep replies private. Never quote a reader publicly without their written OK, and never invent a quote.
- If most say "different price", note the prices they name; change PRO_MONTHLY in src/costwire/pricing.py only after Andy decides.
