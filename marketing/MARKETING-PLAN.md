# Marketing Plan — The Cost Wire
Created 2026-11-07. Nothing here has been sent, posted or paid for. Andy approves in chat.
Rules: data not advice; no fake testimonials, user counts or income claims; NO FFA/schools/colleges (Andy's rule).

## Real buyer
Row-crop and livestock farmers and the ag businesses around them (farm managers, ag lenders, co-op/ag retail agronomists, custom operators, landlords who set cash rent) in WI, IA, IL, MN. They buy fall fertilizer, diesel, feed and rent ground, and want one page showing prices vs 2 weeks and 1 year ago with a source on every number.

## Where they hang out (verify each group's current rules before posting)
- AgTalk forum: https://talk.newagtalk.com/ (farmer forum; check forum rules on links/promo)
- Reddit r/farming: https://www.reddit.com/r/farming/ and r/agriculture (read sidebar self-promo rules first)
- Local farm radio and ag news that already cover input prices: Brownfield Ag News https://www.brownfieldagnews.com/ , RFD-TV https://www.rfdtv.com/ , Farm Week https://www.farmweeknow.com/ (pitch as a free data source, email/contact form only)
- Commodity Wire list (Andy's own sister product): cross-promote.

## Competitors (prices NOT verified — Andy or next run must confirm before we quote any)
1. DTN Retail Fertilizer Trends (https://www.dtnpf.com/): weekly retail fertilizer averages published free in articles; the full DTN Fertilizer Index with state averages is for MyDTN subscribers (per DTN's own article text). Subscription price: not found, do not quote.
2. farmdoc daily (https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/): free university e-newsletter with analysis of input costs. Price: free (not confirmed this run).
Gap we fill: one short weekly page, Midwest-only, USDA/EIA/NASS public data side by side, every number with source link and date, no advice.

## Offer
Free weekly brief (web + email). Later paid tier (~$9-15/month per BRIEF.md): your-state numbers, price alerts, cost-per-acre calculator. Nothing is sold yet.
