## Title
Where I found current Iowa and Illinois fertilizer prices (USDA, with links)

## Post body

I got tired of hunting for fall fertilizer prices in a dozen PDFs, so I pulled the USDA reports into one table. Sharing what's in the latest ones in case it saves you time.

USDA AMS Production Cost Reports, data as of Oct 30, 2026 (average prices, not dealer quotes):

| | Iowa | Illinois |
|---|---|---|
| Anhydrous | $902.33/ton | $981.11/ton |
| UAN 32% | $510.00/ton | $540.00/ton |
| MAP | $976.00/ton | $948.00/ton |
| Potash | $499.80/ton | $517.73/ton |
| Farm diesel | $5.65/gal | $5.71/gal |

Illinois also lists urea at $770.00/ton, UAN 28% at $467.86/ton and DAP at $932.14/ton. Iowa also lists propane at $1.69/gal.

Dried distillers grains (10%), USDA National Weekly Grain Co-Products Report, Nov 2, 2026: IA $170.65, IL $195.00, MN $175.00, WI $174.00 per ton.

Reports:
- Iowa: https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_2863.pdf
- Illinois: https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_3195.pdf
- Co-products: https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_3618.pdf

These are state averages from the government reports. Your dealer's price can be different, so use them as a reference point, not a quote.

What do you see locally compared to these? Curious how far off the averages run from what dealers are quoting in your area.
